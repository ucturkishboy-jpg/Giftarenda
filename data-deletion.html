1. Data Deletion Instructions

At GIF ARENDA, we value your privacy and grant you complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Meta Platform Terms, Telegram Bot guidelines, and international privacy standards, you have the right to request the complete deletion of any personal data associated with your use of our service.

This page provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how users can request permanent deletion of their data from our systems.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request data deletion, you can submit an email request directly to our administrative team. Follow these steps:

  1. Compose an email: Open your email client and compose a message to:
    ucturkishboy@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: Use the subject line:
    Data Deletion Request - GIF ARENDA
  3. Provide your identifier: In the body of the message, provide your identifier so we can locate your records:
    • Your Telegram User ID (e.g., numeric ID) or Telegram @username.
    • Or your account/session identifier used within GIF ARENDA.
  4. Submit the request: Send the email from an address you monitor so we can deliver your deletion confirmation.
Removing Meta/Facebook App Permissions: If you connected to GIF ARENDA through Meta / Facebook, you can also manage your permissions directly in your Facebook profile:
Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, find GIF ARENDA, and click Remove.

3. What Information May Be Deleted

Upon receiving and validating your deletion request, we will remove all associated user records from our active databases, including:

  • Stored Telegram user identifier, username, or display name.
  • Active and historical interaction logs with the GIF ARENDA bot.
  • Cached preference settings and temporary session data.
  • Meta platform account associations or authorization tokens, if applicable.

Note: Minimal transactional or compliance records that are required to be kept by legal, tax, or fraud prevention obligations will be securely retained only for the legally mandated period and permanently purged thereafter.

4. Processing of the Request

We handle all data deletion requests promptly and transparently:

  • Verification: We may verify your identity or ownership of the identifier to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.
  • Processing Time: Requests are processed within 30 calendar days of receipt.
  • Confirmation: Once your data has been completely erased from our active databases, we will send a final confirmation reply to your email address.
